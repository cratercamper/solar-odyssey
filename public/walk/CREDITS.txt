Park people (walk/mobs/mpfb_*.vrm): made for Solar Odyssey in Blender with MPFB 2 (MakeHuman), using the
MakeHuman system assets (CC0 1.0: body, skins, hair, clothes, eyes, eyebrows). Generated from
tools/mobs/mpfb_style.json; hidden body parts under clothes removed, hair recoloured, textures resized.
Motions (walk/motions.json): Mixamo (Adobe) animations, retargeted and converted to compact keyframes for these characters.
