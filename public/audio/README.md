# Audio sources

## apollo17/ — real Apollo 17 mission audio (NASA, public domain)

Cut from recordings published in the Apollo Flight Journal and the Apollo Lunar Surface Journal (apollojournals.org).

- `liftoff.mp3`: Kennedy launch commentary, T-10 s to tower clear (AFJ day 1 clip `a17_t-0000543.mp3`, 333.0–352.9 s)
- `landed.mp3`: "Okay, Houston. The Challenger has landed!" and CapCom's reply, GET 113:02:11 (ALSJ clip `a17a_1124856_to_1130348.mp3`, 793.3–800.5 s)
- `tli.mp3`: "you are Go for TLI" (CapCom Overmyer) and Cernan's reply, GET 002:53:12 (AFJ clip `a17_0024900.mp3`, 249.3–262.8 s)
- `aos.mp3`: "Houston, this is America… America has arrived on station" after the first pass behind the Moon, GET 089:21:18 (AFJ clip `a17_0891530.mp3`, 347.7–360.3 s)
- `pdi.mp3`: powered descent ignition count and "Ignition, Houston", GET 112:49:51 (ALSJ clip `a17a_1124856_to_1130348.mp3`, 57.9–65.8 s)
- `orange.mp3`: "Oh, hey! … It's orange!" — Schmitt finds the orange soil at Shorty crater (Station 4), GET 145:26:22 (ALSJ clip `A17A1452348.mp3`, 161.9–182.3 s)
- `lastwords.mp3`: Cernan's last words on the Moon, "…as we came and, God willing, as we shall return, with peace and hope for all mankind", GET 170:41:36 (ALSJ TV clip `a17v.1703959.mpg`, audio 96.6–114.4 s)
- `ascent.mp3`: ascent count, ignition and "we're on our way", GET 188:01:32 (ALSJ TV clip `a17v_1880127.mpg`, audio 5.0–18.0 s)
- `capture.mp3`: "Capture, Houston." and CapCom's reply at soft dock, GET 003:57:03 (AFJ day 1 clip `a17_0033704.mp3`, 1194.9–1200.0 s)
- `harddock.mp3`: probe retract, "Ka-chunk. By gosh!" and "ripple fire" at hard dock, GET 003:57:20 (AFJ clip `a17_0033704.mp3`, 1216.0–1229.4 s)
- `ejection.mp3`: "on my mark, S-IVB/LM Sep: 3, 2, 1… There she goes. LM came with us", GET 004:45:23 (AFJ clip `a17_0043913.mp3`, 369.4–384.6 s)
- `earthview.mp3`: Cernan on the full Earth, "these kind of views that stick with you forever… Africa dominates the world", GET 005:22:59 (AFJ clip `a17_0051716.mp3`, 342.6–369.6 s)

## planets/ — Gustav Holst, The Planets, Op. 32 (composed 1914–16, public domain)

Recording: Skidmore College Orchestra, provided by Musopen and released as public domain (via Wikimedia Commons, files "Gustav Holst - the planets, op. 32 - iv. jupiter, the bringer of jollity.ogg" and "... i. mars, the bringer of war.ogg").

- `jupiter-opening.mp3`: Jupiter, 0:00–0:58
- `jupiter-hymn.mp3`: Jupiter, 3:23–4:51 (central theme)
- `mars.mp3`: Mars, 0:44–1:48

Recording: "The President's Own" United States Marine Band (a work of the U.S. government, public domain; Wikimedia Commons files 'HOLST "The Planets, Op. 32 5. Saturn, the Bringer of Old Age" - "The President's Own" U.S. Marine Band.opus' and '... 7. Neptune, the Mystic ...'). Loudness raised to match the other pieces.

- `saturn.mp3`: Saturn, 0:00–1:50 (fade out)
- `neptune.mp3`: Neptune, 0:00–1:50 (fade out)

## classics/ — other public-domain works with a space feel (junji 2026-11-22)

Each piece is used once in the Moon mission. Files fetched from Wikimedia Commons; loudness adjusted.

- `blue-danube.mp3`: Johann Strauss II, An der schönen blauen Donau, 0:00–1:40. Recording: European Archive via Musopen, CC0 (Commons "Strauss, An der schönen blauen Donau.ogg").
- `zarathustra.mp3`: Richard Strauss, Also sprach Zarathustra, introduction (Sonnenaufgang), complete. Recording: Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0) — attribution required (Commons "Also Sprach Zarathustra - Einleitung.ogg").
- `clair-de-lune.mp3`: Claude Debussy, Suite bergamasque: Clair de lune, 0:00–1:50. Performed by Laurens Goedhart (piano), 2011, public domain (Commons "Clair de lune (Claude Debussy) Suite bergamasque.ogg").
- `new-world-largo.mp3`: Antonín Dvořák, Symphony No. 9 "From the New World", II. Largo, 0:00–2:05. Recording via Musopen, public domain (Commons "Antonin Dvorak - symphony no. 9 in e minor 'from the new world', op. 95 - ii. largo.ogg").
